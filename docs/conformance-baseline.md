# Conformance baseline — the invariants nobody writes down

friday's shipped catalog of baseline invariants (INC-105 FR-105.4): the handful of rules no project writes in its own documents and every live service needs, seeded switched-on for every project (D3) at the fuller-list sizing the PM chose over the author's scarred-only recommendation (D4 — an overrule, recorded as such).
The grammar, the closed `when:` vocabulary, the decline path and the sweep that runs these lines are all single-homed at `docs/contracts/conformance-envelope.md` § The baseline line grammar — cited, never restated here.
What the marks mean, because most of this list is plausibility (KH-6): `scarred — <finding>` names the real audit finding behind a rule; `unscarred` says plainly that nothing but plausibility is behind it yet. The judged answers these rules collect across real runs are the data the demotion question waits for (§10 of the increment); nothing here retires a rule for firing noise.
Deliberately absent, because each already has its one home: secret values and dotenv posture (`tools/secret_posture_check.py`, INC-204), dependency advisories (`tools/dep_advisories.py`, INC-103), and the generic code-health numbers (`tools/maintainability_measure.py`, INC-008). A rule with an owner is cited from its owner, never re-swept here.
Declining an invariant that does apply is a written accepted risk with its reason — the rule-shaped ledger entry at location `project-wide`, aging back through the deep clean like every accepted risk (D3; the shape: `docs/contracts/standards-deviation.md`).
